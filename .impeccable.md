## Design Context

### Users
Established and growing businesses (service companies, trades, salons, event rental, retail, advisory firms) whose growth is held back by missed calls, manual admin, disconnected tools or a website that doesn't sell. Orryvelle is a design and engineering studio based in Bulgaria, working with clients worldwide, in English and Bulgarian. It sells three equal disciplines: Custom Software, Enterprise Solutions (AI agents, automation, integrations) and Websites. None is a supporting service, and no order is fixed.

### Brand personality
Direct, premium, practical and confident: a problem-solver, not a tech list. Explain the actual work a system does without unsupported revenue promises, claims of total autonomy, invented proof or prices.

### Aesthetic direction
Orryvelle's own look: graphite ink, mineral parchment paper and brushed bronze, condensed uppercase display type (Barlow Condensed with DM Sans; Fira Sans Extra Condensed with Onest for Bulgarian), rectangular controls, crosshair corner marks and framed imagery. bycrawford.com is the reference for structure and level of detail only (mega-menu, case-study template, about page), never for its palette, fonts or copy. Avoid generic dashboards, fake metrics and effects without a job.

### Principles
1. Give the three disciplines equal weight in every section, menu and piece of copy.
2. Let visitors recognise their problem first, then show what gets built and how it works.
3. Show real work only; label concept studies clearly, and claim only what the work itself shows.
4. Keep booking a free 30-minute call one step away on every page.
5. Keep the English and Bulgarian versions identical in structure; Bulgarian copy uses the polite "вие".
6. Preserve the enquiry and booking flows, mobile layouts, keyboard access, AA contrast and reduced-motion behaviour (the owner's Mac has reduce motion on, so nothing may look broken with it).
7. Speed is a feature: no new client JavaScript or fonts on every page without measuring first.
