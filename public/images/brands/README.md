# Local brand marks

21 SVG files, about 48 KB combined. Downloaded 2026-09-25.

The exact asset URLs are recorded in `sources.json`. Most marks come from the SVGL collection (https://github.com/pheralb/svgl); HubSpot, Xero, and Airtable come from VectorLogoZone (https://www.vectorlogo.zone/). The SVGL collection license is retained in `LICENSE-svgl.txt`. Individual brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 are represented by the Google and Microsoft vendor marks. The site uses these to identify example integrations, not to imply a partnership or endorsement. No recreated letter placeholders or runtime logo APIs are used.

Assets keep their original vector paths and colors. Optical sizing is applied in BrandLogo.tsx. The images are eager-loaded because they are small, reused, and should be ready before entering the carousel viewport.
